Animations on the cue track

Each animation shows as a strip in the bottom third of its cue block. The strip's left/right edges are its start/end on the same time axis as the track, and its fill is the legend — a color change renders the actual gradient, alpha renders an opacity ramp, typewriter a left-to-right wipe. Click once selects the cue; click again on a strip focuses that animation (its edges become drag handles). Use the controls to scrub, zoom, and toggle the muted/active treatment.
