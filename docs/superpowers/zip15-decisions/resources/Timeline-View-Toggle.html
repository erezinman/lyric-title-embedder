Bars are constant height; past 3 a ＋N toggle lifts a floating overlay — the lane height never changes. Separate H / V zoom; one continuous playhead.

▍ Verdict

Ship the density toggle — Compact · Coherent · Lanes are three stops on one axis (most compact → most separated), defaulting to Coherent. One shared tl-anim renderer; constant-height bars; overflow past MAX_VISIBLE=3 opens a floating overlay (accordion, click-away / Esc to close) so the lane never reflows.

This matters most with many groups: Lanes becomes unusably tall, Compact scatters a group across rows, and Coherent keeps each group readable as one line at a fraction of the height. The toggle FLIP-animates between all three and keeps the selected cue pinned. Confirm: auto-pick the default by event count (Lanes when ≤~6, Coherent above)?

Try it: step Compact → Coherent → Lanes and watch the rows reflow (8 events here — the many-group case). Click a cue's ＋N for its overlay; drag H/V to zoom.
