Cue visibility — the window vs. when the text is actually on screen

The block's left/right edges are the event window [win_start, win_end]. But the text's real appear / disappear can differ: a fade-in delays the appear, a fade-out clears it early, and linger holds it on screen past the window. These markers show that real visible span. Toggle how often they show.

Show ticks on Auto — render the appear/disappear markers only when they differ from the window edge. Most cues appear at win_start and clear at win_end, so their block edge already says it; drawing ticks there is noise. The markers earn their place exactly when there's a delay, early clear, or linger — which is also when an editor most needs to see it.
